Core Technology

What is structuring? Giving meaning a shape machines can read

We build the mechanism that produces correct data.

The shared design principle is Human-in-the-loop (HITL): experts who know each field firsthand check and approve the primary information. Instead of AI guesses, we build the mechanism itself that produces verified primary data, or VAD (Verified Authentic Data).

Message from the CEO

Shingo Takahashi, CEO
The more AI advances, the more the responsibility for defining meaning comes back to people.

We do not assume a future in which AI can do everything. On the contrary, we believe that the deeper AI goes into society, the more serious the problems of errors, ambiguity, and accountability become.

This is not just a story about technology. It is a challenge in how to design the premises of the next society.
