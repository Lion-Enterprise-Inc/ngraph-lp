Security

Don't show. Don't break. Don't input.

We don't rely on settings alone. Three layers, including a posted sheet and daily habits.

Settings

Don't show

Folders with HR, payroll, bank accounts and personal data are set so AI cannot read them. We test that it works on the spot by naming them directly.

Settings

Don't break

Delete and reset commands are blocked. Anything sent outside asks a person every time. Inside the work folder, "go back to how it was" restores it.

Posted sheet and habits

Don't input

"What you may and may not enter" goes on one sheet posted in the office. AI answers are drafts. A person checks numbers and dates.

Text you send goes to the AI company's servers. So people decide what goes in.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we sign up if no one on our team knows much about AI?

Yes. That is what this set is for. Just name one person in charge; they do not need to be an expert. Users simply ask in plain Japanese, in the app window they normally use.

Do we need to decide in advance which tasks AI will handle?

No. We set it up first, complete one real task in the training session, and find the next ones as you use it.

Do we need a ChatGPT or Claude subscription?

Yes. The generative AI service is contracted in your company's name. Usage fees are not included in the set. We decide together which one to subscribe to in the first online consultation.

How many PCs can you set up?

Up to five. The PCs being set up must allow software installation.

How long does it take?

About 5-7 business days from start to finish. The actual schedule is set in the first online consultation to suit your business. It can also be split into 1.5-hour sessions per day.

How much does it cost?

¥220,000 all-in (tax included). The price does not grow with time; it is a one-time purchase with no monthly fee. Travel costs are extra. Individual items are not offered separately.

Which areas do you visit?

Mainly Hokuriku (Fukui, Ishikawa, Toyama), and we also visit Osaka and Kyoto. Travel costs are extra. For other areas, please ask.

Can we use a subsidy?

It depends on the program. If your subsidy application needs a cost breakdown, we will prepare one separately.

Can you take on a larger scope (custom workflows, system integration)?

That is a separate service. See AI Adoption Support (FDE), where we work on-site and build AI into each workflow. Additional support after the starter set is also available on request.
