Our Mission

Implementing the mechanism by which correct data is born.

NGraph develops technology that structures food, companies, and people — the most complex and unstructured data domains in the world — into a form AI can handle correctly. We have expanded the methods refined in our first proving ground, food, to company evaluation and human evaluation.

The shared design philosophy is Human-in-the-loop (HITL), in which experts who know each domain's front line confirm and approve the first-party information. We implement the very mechanism that produces verified first-party data — VAD (Verified Authentic Data) — rather than AI's guesses.

The more AI advances, the more "the responsibility to define meaning" returns to humans. What to delegate to AI, and what humans must carry — we are a company that answers that, through design and implementation.

A message from the CEO

The more AI advances, the more "the responsibility to define meaning" returns to humans.

We do not assume a future in which AI becomes all-powerful. On the contrary, we believe that the deeper AI embeds itself in society, the more serious the problems of error, ambiguity, and accountability become.

NGraph is a company that answers, through design and implementation, "what to delegate to AI and what humans must carry." Starting from food — at once everyday, cultural, and high-risk — we are building the right relationship between AI and humans.

This is not merely a story about technology. It is the challenge of how to design the "premise" of the next society.
