DEFINITION

Turn information into the company's record.

Organizing is not the goal. The goal is to put the company's facts and decisions in a state ready for the next decision.

"Whose information?" × "What state is it in?" Two axes put AI and people on the same standard.

ABC sorting×Status sorting

ABC sorting | Whose information Status sorting | What state
What is settled What is in motion
A. Us Rules, products, roles, decisions Sales, costs, where each project stands
B. Counterparts Customer details, contacts, contracts Deals, requests, where each relationship stands
C. External Regulations, laws, known competitors Upcoming changes, competitor moves, deadlines

Three rules for saving to the record

  1. Keep the sourceEvidence, date checked, who updated. Unsettled items are marked "needs confirmation".
  2. One current stateThe latest version is current; older information moves to history.
  3. Secrets: location onlyFor personal data and credentials, record only where they are kept and who has access.

Even when the person changes, even when the AI changes, the basis for decisions remains.That is the company record.
