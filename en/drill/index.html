Approach

The goal is to keep your company running.

An expensive product is not always the answer. In STEP 2, we look at how you work and choose a combination of measures like these.

Keep less, limit access

After checking legal retention duties and business needs, clear out data you don't need to keep. Limit who can view and operate things to only those who need to.

Keep offline backups

Backups kept off the network, and a check, using data we agree on, that you can actually restore from them.

Work on paper if needed

Keep emergency contacts and fallback procedures for orders and shipping on paper too, and decide where the paper is kept and how it is updated.

Prepare your people

Based on the training email results, share with staff how to spot suspicious emails and how to report them.

Only the products you need, only where needed

We start by improving operations and reviewing the settings of your existing systems. Any additional products are chosen by comparing effect, cost and operating burden (our own products are judged by the same standard).

Safety

We agree in writing before we start.

  • We confirm admin rights and permission, and work only within the scope agreed in writing
  • We decide in advance when to stop and how to roll back
  • Training emails never ask staff to enter real passwords or similar information
  • The results also state what we could not do and what we did not check

Not included: the cost of equipment or products, large-scale system overhauls, 24-hour monitoring, and emergency recovery after an incident.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we stop after STEP 1?

Yes. We deliver the report and improvement plan in a form you can use whether you fix things yourselves or hire another company.

Will this disrupt our work?

We follow procedures that keep the impact low, but we cannot guarantee zero impact. Before we start, we agree in writing on when to stop and how to roll back.

Do you have a track record?

We are just starting the pilot, so we have no results we can publish yet. That is why we are seeking pilot partners, limited to companies in Fukui Prefecture.

How much does it cost?

For STEP 1 (reconnaissance, simulated attack, report), we ask about the scope, such as how many sites you have, and give you the price during the free consultation. For STEP 2 (on-site fixes, retest), we quote when we deliver the report, based on the STEP 1 results.

Can companies outside Fukui Prefecture apply?

During the pilot period, we are limiting this to companies in Fukui Prefecture.

We are under attack right now. Can you help?

Immediate incident response itself (containing the breach and restoring systems) is outside our scope. To stop the damage from spreading, please contact the police cyber desk or the IPA (Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan) first. After things settle, we can help investigate the cause and run a prevention-focused assessment, so feel free to reach out to us as well.

Can we also ask about how we use AI internally?

Yes. If you want to use AI without sending company information to outside AI services, see the Local AI Pack. If you want to build AI into your work, see AI Adoption Support (FDE).
