FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Isn't it weaker than cloud AI?

It falls short of the very latest models, but it is good enough for defined tasks. After installation, we check what it can do using your actual documents inside your company before you start using it.

What if the hardware breaks?

It is repaired under the manufacturer's warranty (DGX Spark has a 1-year pickup repair warranty). We arrange the repair as part of maintenance.

How do updates work if it is not online?

We check OS and model updates before installing them. For offline setups, we visit and install them on site.

How much does it cost?

There are three plans based on the number of users. The initial cost, hardware included, is about ¥1,640,000 to ¥2,830,000 (estimate, excluding tax), and maintenance starts at ¥50,000 per month (excluding tax). Hardware prices are estimates as of September 2026 and change with market prices.

What if cloud AI is enough for us?

In the free briefing, we ask about the information you handle and your internal rules, and decide with you whether cloud AI is enough or the AI should be kept in-house. If cloud AI is enough, we can also introduce our Generative AI Starter Set.

Can you also customize it for our work?

Yes. It can be combined with AI Adoption Support (FDE), where we work on site and build AI into each task.
